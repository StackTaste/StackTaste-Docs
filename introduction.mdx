StackTaste is the persistent taste layer for coding agents. Preferences, conventions, policies, and lessons live as Markdown in the repo and as history in the cloud.

Local `.stacktaste/` is what the agent reads. Cloud is the team copy, the dashboard, and the audit log.

## Live now

- **Sip** ($1 / month) and **Stack** ($9 / month or $90 / year), billed on the organization
- Dashboard at `/{org}/{project}` for preferences, policies, lessons, members, and keys
- Org billing and invoices at `/projects/billing`
- CLI: login, init, sync, **taste / list**, learn, review, check
- MCP tools plus Cursor hooks, including **StackTaste Alert** on blocked tools
- Ranked context and generated `.cursor/rules`
